Research and reuse boundaries

Provenance before inference

Prefer a direct, current repository read; preserve the revision, source, evidence status, and license. Historical pins remain historical.

Consent and personal methodology

Public work may be studied within applicable permissions. Public access alone is not consent to fingerprint, archive, or reconstruct a person's identifying methods.

Proposal is not deployment

Blueprints, gate texts, prototype checks, and proposed architecture do not establish an implemented or activated control.

Source-first public navigation · Research statuses are project-specific · Private material is excluded.
